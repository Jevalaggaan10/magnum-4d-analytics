# Research-to-change register

Checked 2026-11-03 against primary pages; no claim of an exhaustive survey or a
reproduced Malaysian lottery advantage. Metadata/full text access below describes
what was inspected, not an independent replication. Engineering choices are
inferences from these sources and this repository.

| Source, authors, version/status/access | Task / evaluation / useful result and limit | Change / falsification experiment |
|---|---|---|
| [Proper scoring rules for estimation and forecast evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01781), Kartik Waghmare & Johanna Ziegel, v4 2026-05-10, review preprint; full HTML | Probability estimation/evaluation; proper losses reward honest distributions, not winning-number evidence | Full categorical log loss/Brier; reject model usefulness without forward loss gain |
| [On Over-fitting in Model Selection and Subsequent Selection Bias](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v11/cawley10a.html), Gavin Cawley & Nicola Talbot, JMLR 2010; publisher abstract/full paper available | Model-selection experiments show selection bias can rival algorithm gains; not lottery evidence | Inner chronological selection, frozen outer procedure; future checkpoint rejects exploratory optimism |
| [Beyond IID: How General Are Tabular Foundation Models, Really?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30410), Lennart Purucker, Andrej Tschalzev et al., v2 2026-10-30, accepted NeurIPS Datasets/Benchmarks; abstract/metadata | 142 datasets, 11 models across IID/temporal/grouped settings; benchmark strengths do not transfer automatically | Draw-group chronological splits; reject generalization when frozen future loss fails |
| [Experimental Limits of Machine Learning on Near-Random Data](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-30518-3_23), Alexandru Dinu, Sara-Gabriela Modan & Mădălin Frunzete, first online 2026-06-30, conference chapter; abstract only, full chapter paywalled | 2510 Romanian 6/49 draws, temporally separated frequency/Markov/MLP/RF; abstract reports no significant gain.
